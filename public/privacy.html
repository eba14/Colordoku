Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Colordoku is a free, single-purpose puzzle game. This page explains what information is collected when you optionally sign in, and how it's used.

Playing without an account

You do not need to sign in to play. Without an account, no personal information is collected at all — the puzzle runs entirely in your browser.

What's collected if you sign in

Signing in (via email/password or "Continue with Google") is optional and only used to save your progress and best times across sessions and devices. If you sign in, the following is stored, associated with your account:

  • Your email address (and, for Google sign-in, your name and profile picture, as shared by Google)
  • Your best completion time for each difficulty mode
  • Your current in-progress puzzle, if you leave a game unfinished, so you can resume it later

Where it's stored

This data is stored with Supabase, a database and authentication provider. Access is restricted so that only you can read or write your own data — no other player can see it.

What's not collected

Colordoku does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies for tracking. It does not sell or share your data with third parties. Signing in with Google only grants access to your basic profile info (name, email, avatar) — nothing else in your Google account is accessed.

Deleting your data

You can delete your saved best times at any time from the in-game "Clear My Best Times" option on the menu. To delete your account entirely, email the address below.
